# Software Advice — default product listing copy

Paste-ready. Written for the Gartner Digital Markets portal (Software Advice /
Capterra / GetApp), using the vocabulary their category reviewers check against
rather than the wording used on the marketing site.

Deliberate omissions, so nothing here can be refused the way the Hospitality LMS
request was: no point of sale, no customer invoicing, no course or training
content claims. Every capability below is in the shipped product.

---

## Short description

> Rotahr is all-in-one operations software for independent restaurants, bars,
> cafés and hotels. Staff scheduling, table reservations, HACCP food safety
> records, stock and recipe costing, supplier ordering, receipt scanning and
> payroll hours live in one place instead of six. Built by a former chef, not a
> software company.

---

## Long description

> Rotahr was built by Gabor Nemeth, who spent years cooking professionally in
> Ireland before writing a line of it. The rota lived on a laminated sheet, the
> food safety records in a folder nobody opened until the inspector arrived.
>
> It is made for the operator who is also covering a section on a Friday night,
> so everything is designed to be finished on a phone, between tasks, by someone
> nobody has trained on it.
>
> **Scheduling and labour cost.** Build the rota and watch wage cost update as
> you drag shifts, so you learn you are over budget while you can still fix it.
> Publish to your team's phones. Swap requests route through a manager, not a
> group chat. Availability and approved time off sit in the view you build
> against, and late or no-show alerts fire when someone has not clocked in.
>
> **Time, attendance and payroll hours.** Staff clock in and out on their phones,
> and rostered hours sit beside actual hours so the gap shows before payroll
> rather than after. Break entitlement is tracked against working time rules.
> Tips and tronc are distributed and recorded.
>
> **Table reservations.** A visual floor plan laid out to match the actual room,
> with tables placed, shaped, sized and given a capacity. Status updates live for
> the date you are viewing, and clicking a free table starts a booking already
> assigned to it. Guest profiles build themselves from reservations, including
> visit counts and no-show history, and a public booking page takes reservations
> directly.
>
> **HACCP and food safety.** Paperless temperature checks against your own named
> fridges, freezers and hot holding units. Cooking and cooling records with the
> thresholds and use-by dates built in. Opening, closing and cleaning checklists
> managers can edit per venue. Reminders follow the shift and repeat until the
> check is actually logged, and one tap produces the inspection pack.
>
> **Stock, costing, ordering and bookkeeping.** Photograph a delivery note and
> its line items populate stock levels, your expense record and your HACCP
> delivery check at once. Recipe costs update from the prices that actually
> landed, so gross margin per dish stays current. Build order lists per supplier,
> move them draft to sent to received, and export them. Upload a supplier
> statement to have it reconciled against what you ordered, with discrepancies
> flagged. Receipts scan the same way, feeding profit and loss, a tax summary and
> CSV export for your accountant.
>
> **What Rotahr is not.** It is not a point of sale, does not process customer
> payments or take orders at the table, and does not host course or training
> content. Rotahr sits alongside your till rather than replacing it.
>
> Runs in any browser and as an iOS and Android app, with push notifications for
> published rotas, shift changes and due food safety checks. Supports EUR, GBP,
> USD, CAD and AUD, with tax wording matching your country. Support comes from
> the founder directly, with no ticket queue in the way. First month free, no
> card required.

## Target market

> Independent hospitality operators and small groups: restaurants, bars,
> gastropubs, cafés, and hotels with food and beverage service. Typically four
> to thirty staff on one site, scaling to multi-venue groups run from a single
> account.
>
> The clearest fit is the owner-operator or general manager who still works
> service, currently running the business across a paper rota, a WhatsApp
> group, a folder of food safety sheets and a shoebox of receipts, and who
> wants one system instead of a subscription for each of those jobs.
>
> Used across Ireland, the UK, the US, Canada and Australia. Less suited to
> large chains and franchises needing deep point-of-sale integration or
> centralised procurement.
