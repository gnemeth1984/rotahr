# Ready-to-send pitches

Copy, adjust, send from `gnemeth1984@gmail.com` or `hello@rotahr.com` — **not**
`sales@rotahr.com`. An editor who sees a sales address treats the mail as
advertising and forwards it to the ad department, which is where pitches go to
die.

Send them **one at a time, spread over days**. These are individuals, not a list.

Mark each one `sent` in Admin → Links when it goes out. That schedules the
follow-up automatically.

## Directory submissions — do these first, they're free and quick

No pitching needed. Under an hour total.

1. **Capterra** — already live. Get the `/p/<id>/Rotahr/` URL out of the
   confirmation email and paste it to me; the footer link and schema markup are
   already built and waiting.
2. **GetApp + Software Advice** — check the existing Gartner vendor portal
   login first. The Capterra submission often syndicates to both, in which case
   this is zero work.
3. **AlternativeTo** — list as an alternative to 7shifts, Deputy, RotaCloud.
   Matches the `/compare` pages we already publish.
4. **SaaSHub** — free form, fast approval.
5. **Crunchbase** — free company profile. Fill it as solo-founded; don't imply
   incorporation or funding that doesn't exist.
6. **SourceForge** business software — free vendor listing.

**Hold back:**

- **G2** — claim the profile, but it ranks on review count and we have one real
  trial user. Low yield until there are paying venues.
- **Product Hunt** — one shot, can't be repeated. Wait for paying customers and
  a demo video. Warm up the maker profile for a few weeks first.

## What not to do

- **Don't buy the €149/mo package.** Six of the seven services are already
  built and running. The seventh — link building — at that price is usually
  directory spam or PBN placements, which Google's link spam update discounts
  at best and penalises at worst.
- **Don't pitch The Caterer yet.** Highest authority in the list, and you get
  one cold introduction. Save it for a real announcement — a named customer,
  funding, or original data.
- **Don't automate any of this.** Each contact converts once.
